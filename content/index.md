Welcome to Prior Art! This is a wiki about prior art in software engineering and computing, where I read research so you don't have to.

I've been writing software for a decade or so and have realized that virtually all of the problems we have are already solved. We often just don't know the answers because it's impossible to know everything. My goal here is to collect solutions to solved problems to try to save us all some time. If I can prevent just one wheel from being reinvented I will consider this endeavour a success.
